How it works

From your voice to their language in four steps

  1. Choose your languages

    Pick the language you speak and the one you want to translate into.

  2. Speak or type

    Press the microphone and talk, or type your message into the text box.

  3. AI translates

    Your speech is transcribed and translated securely by our AI services.

  4. Listen and share

    Play the translation aloud, copy it, or find it later in your history.
